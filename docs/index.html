About this model

Data

6 Panasonic VL coin cells (VL1220 / VL2020 form factors) cycled by Neware at 0.1C/0.2C and room temperature / 40°C. Each cell was cycled for 100 full charge/discharge cycles with ~30s sampling. Cycle 1 is excluded from all analysis and charts: it carries an extra leading rest step not present in any other cycle and its discharge capacity is visibly inconsistent with the rest of the curve on at least one cell — an artifact of test setup, not real aging.

SOH definition

SOH(cycle) = discharge capacity(cycle) / Cref × 100, where Cref is the peak discharge capacity observed in cycles 2–11 (post break-in/formation) — these coin cells show a real capacity rise during early formation cycles before fade begins.

Model & validation

A small residual LSTM (16 hidden units) predicts next-cycle SOH from a 10-cycle window of summary features (capacity, coulombic efficiency, step durations, temperature, voltage), conditioned on C-rate/temperature/form-factor. Validated with leave-one-cell-out cross-validation: mean absolute error of 0.79% SOH on cells never seen during training — beating both a naive persistence baseline (1.12%) and a Random Forest baseline (2.26%). All inference runs locally in your browser via ONNX Runtime Web; no data leaves your device.
